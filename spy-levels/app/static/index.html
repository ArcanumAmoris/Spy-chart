    How to use this

    Range day (price above the gamma flip)

    Dealers hedge against the move: they sell into rallies and buy into dips. Price tends to bounce between walls.

    Fade the edges. Buy near support, sell near resistance, with a stop about $0.30 past the level.

    Trend day (price below the gamma flip)

    Dealers hedge with the move, which pushes it further. Levels break more easily.

    Follow breaks that close past a level on at least 2x average volume. Target the next level.

    Reading the page

    The yellow banner appears when SPY is within $0.25 of a level. Vol pace compares the last 5 minutes with the session average: above 2 means a break is more likely to hold.

    Walls come from open interest, so they shift overnight. Check the map each morning.
